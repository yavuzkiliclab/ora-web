2. Scope and applicable law

This policy explains how we handle personal data of visitors to this international website. Because we serve patients located in the European Union and elsewhere, we aim to align with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies to you, alongside the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Law No. 6698), to which we are subject as a Turkish clinic.

Health information is treated as a special category of personal data under both GDPR (Article 9) and KVKK. We process it only where a lawful basis applies, such as your explicit consent or the provision of healthcare you request.

3. What data we collect

When you contact us through this website, we may process:

  • Identity and contact details you choose to share (for example, your name and the messaging handle or number you write from);
  • The content of your enquiry, which may include information about your dental needs. You decide how much health information to share with us;
  • Technical data such as your device and browser type, and basic usage information, to the extent your browser transmits it.

This website does not host a payment form and does not itself collect payment card data.

4. How we use your data

We use your data to respond to your enquiry, to plan and coordinate the dental treatment and travel arrangements you ask about, and to communicate with you about your request. We do not sell your personal data.
