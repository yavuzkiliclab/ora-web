Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning dental treatment with Ora Clinic in Istanbul. If your question is not here, message us on WhatsApp and we will answer in your language.

How many days will I need in Istanbul?

It depends on your treatment. Veneers and crowns are usually completed within a single visit of several days, while implants are typically planned over two visits with a healing period in between. Once our dentists review your case, we confirm a personal schedule so you can plan flights and time off with confidence.

Can I get a treatment plan and estimate before I travel?

Yes. Share your photos or recent x-rays with us on WhatsApp, and our dentists prepare a proposed treatment route before you book any flight. This gives you a clear idea of the likely steps and scope in advance. The precise plan and estimate are confirmed at your in-person clinical examination in Istanbul, as Turkish health regulation requires, because the final assessment depends on examining your case directly.

Will language be a problem?

No. Our coordinators answer in your language from your very first message, and an interpreter accompanies you at the clinic. Nothing is lost between you and your dentist, so you can ask questions and understand every step of your treatment.

What is included in a treatment package?

Packages are designed to cover the full journey, not just the dental work: treatment, airport transfers, hotel coordination, and an interpreter by your side. Because every treatment is different, the exact inclusions are confirmed with the clinic before you travel, so you know precisely what is arranged.

Which implant and material brands do you use?

Ora works only with globally certified dental brands. The specific brand used in your treatment is selected by your dentist based on your individual case, and it is discussed with you beforehand so you understand what is being placed and why.

Is the clinic officially licensed and accredited?

Yes. Ora operates as a licensed clinic and works within Turkish health tourism regulation. Our certifications and official approvals are available for review, and we are glad to share them if you would like to see them before you travel.

What happens if I need a follow-up after I return home?

Your care does not end when you fly back. Our team stays in touch for aftercare, and any follow-up is coordinated remotely in your language, so distance does not leave you without support.

How do I arrange travel, hotel, and pickup?

Once your treatment dates are set, your coordinator arranges airport pickup and helps organize your hotel stay. The aim is that you arrive with everything already in place and can focus only on your treatment.

How do I start?

Message us on WhatsApp in your own language. A coordinator replies, reviews your case, and your treatment journey begins with a single message. There is no obligation, and you can ask as many questions as you need before deciding anything.
