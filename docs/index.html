/ 05 · FAQ
How many days will I need in Istanbul?

It depends on the treatment. Veneers and crowns are usually completed within a single visit of several days; implants are typically planned over two visits with a healing period in between. Your personal schedule is confirmed after your case is reviewed.

Will language be a problem?

No. Our coordinators answer in your language and an interpreter accompanies you at the clinic, so nothing is lost between you and your dentist.

What is included in a treatment package?

Packages are designed to cover the full journey: treatment, airport transfers, hotel coordination, and an interpreter by your side. Exact inclusions are confirmed with the clinic before you travel.

How do I start?

Message us on WhatsApp in your own language. A coordinator replies, reviews your case, and your journey begins with a single message.
