/ 05 · FAQ
How many days will I need in Istanbul?

It depends on the treatment. Veneers and crowns are usually completed within a single visit of several days; implants are typically planned over two visits with a healing period in between. Your personal schedule is confirmed after your case is reviewed.

Can I get a plan before I travel?

Yes. Share your photos or x-rays on WhatsApp and our dentists prepare a proposed treatment route before you book any flight. The final plan is confirmed at your clinical examination in Istanbul, as Turkish health regulation requires.

Will language be a problem?

No. Our coordinators answer in your language and an interpreter accompanies you at the clinic, so nothing is lost between you and your dentist.

How do I get a treatment plan and estimate?

Send your photos or recent x-rays on WhatsApp. Our dentists review them and prepare a proposed treatment route. A precise plan and estimate are confirmed at your in-person clinical examination in Istanbul.

What is included in a treatment package?

Packages are designed to cover the full journey: treatment, airport transfers, hotel coordination, and an interpreter by your side. Exact inclusions are confirmed with the clinic before you travel.

Which implant and material brands do you use?

Ora works only with globally certified dental brands. The specific brand used in your treatment is selected by your dentist based on your case and discussed with you beforehand.

Is the clinic officially licensed and accredited?

Ora operates as a licensed clinic and works within Turkish health tourism regulation. Certifications and official approvals are available for review.

What happens if I need a follow-up after I return home?

Your care does not end when you fly back. Our team stays in touch for aftercare, and follow-up is coordinated remotely in your language.

How do I arrange travel, hotel, and pickup?

Once your treatment dates are set, your coordinator arranges airport pickup and helps organize your hotel stay, so you arrive with everything in place.

How do I start?

Message us on WhatsApp in your own language. A coordinator replies, reviews your case, and your journey begins with a single message.
