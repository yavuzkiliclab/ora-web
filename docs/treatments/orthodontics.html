Orthodontics

Orthodontics straightens teeth and improves the way they meet, using fixed braces or clear aligners. It is available for adults and teenagers, and is planned around both function and appearance, so that teeth not only look better but also work together well.

Who it may suit

Orthodontic treatment is often considered by people with crowded, spaced, or misaligned teeth, or with a bite that does not meet comfortably. Whether braces or aligners are suitable, and which option fits your case, is determined by your dentist after examination.

How it usually works

  1. Assessment and planning. Your dentist assesses your teeth and plans the movement, often with digital tools for aligners.
  2. Fitting. Fixed braces are fitted, or your first set of clear aligners is provided.
  3. Progress. Treatment continues over time, with adjustments or new aligners as the teeth move.
  4. Retention. Once alignment is complete, retainers help hold the result.

What to expect

Orthodontic treatment usually takes place over a longer period than most other dental treatments, and much of it can often be followed remotely between visits, which suits international patients. Timing depends on your case, and your dentist will explain what is realistic. Results vary from person to person.

Recovery and aftercare

Braces and aligners can feel unusual at first, and some tightness is normal after adjustments or when moving to a new aligner. Good oral hygiene is especially important during orthodontic treatment. After treatment, wearing retainers as advised is the main way to protect the result, and your dentist will explain how and when to wear them.

Common questions about orthodontics

Braces or clear aligners: which is better?

Both can straighten teeth, and the right choice depends on your case and preferences. Your dentist will explain the options after examining you.

How long does treatment take?

Orthodontic treatment usually takes place over a longer period than other dental work, but the exact length depends on your case. Your dentist will give you an idea after assessment.

Can treatment be followed remotely?

Much of orthodontic treatment, particularly with clear aligners, can often be monitored remotely between visits. Your dentist will explain how follow-up works for your plan.

Do I need to wear a retainer afterwards?

Retainers are usually needed to hold the result after treatment. Your dentist will explain how and how long to wear them.

Related treatments

Orthodontics is sometimes the first step in a wider plan. Once teeth are aligned, aesthetic dentistry may refine the smile further. Healthy gums, cared for through periodontology, are important during and after treatment.
