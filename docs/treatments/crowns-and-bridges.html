Crowns and Bridges

A crown is a restoration that covers a damaged or weakened tooth to protect it and restore its shape. A bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the teeth or implants on either side. Both are often made from zirconia or ceramic, chosen to match your natural teeth in strength and appearance.

Who they may suit

Crowns and bridges are often considered when a tooth is significantly damaged, worn, or weakened, after root canal treatment, or to replace one or more missing teeth. Whether they are the right option for you depends on the condition of the tooth, the surrounding teeth, and your gums, and is determined by your dentist after examination.

How it usually works

  1. Assessment and shade matching. Your dentist assesses the teeth and selects a material and shade to match your smile.
  2. Preparation. The tooth is prepared and an impression or digital scan is taken.
  3. Fabrication. The crown or bridge is made, often in a dental laboratory.
  4. Fitting. The restoration is fitted and adjusted for comfort and appearance.

What to expect

Treatment timing depends on your case and is confirmed by your dentist. A temporary restoration is sometimes provided while the final one is made. Comfort, fit, and appearance are checked with you before the restoration is finalised, and results vary from person to person.

Recovery and aftercare

Some sensitivity can be normal for a short time after a crown or bridge is fitted. Your dentist will give you guidance on caring for the restoration, which usually includes good daily oral hygiene, cleaning around the restoration as advised, and regular check-ups. With good care, crowns and bridges are designed to be a durable, long-term restoration.

Common questions about crowns and bridges

What is the difference between a crown and a bridge?

A crown covers and protects a single tooth, while a bridge replaces one or more missing teeth by anchoring to the teeth or implants beside the gap. Your dentist will explain which suits your case.

What materials are used?

Crowns and bridges are often made from zirconia or ceramic, selected to match your natural teeth. The choice depends on your case and is discussed with you beforehand.

How long do crowns and bridges last?

This depends on the material, the health of the surrounding teeth and gums, and how well they are cared for. Your dentist can explain what supports longevity in your situation.

Do I need a crown after root canal treatment?

A tooth is often restored with a crown after root canal treatment to protect it, but whether this applies to you is determined by your dentist.

Related treatments

Crowns and bridges are often part of a wider plan. A crown may be placed on a dental implant to replace a missing tooth, or after root canal treatment to protect a tooth. Where appearance is the main goal, they may be planned alongside aesthetic dentistry.
